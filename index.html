06 — FAQ

QUESTIONS I GET

01Are you a data scientist or an infrastructure engineer?

Both, and it's the same job at different altitudes. Models fail in production for infrastructure reasons far more often than statistical ones, so knowing the provisioning and deployment layer makes the modelling work better, not worse.

02Why so much automation?

Manual work doesn't scale, doesn't audit well and never behaves the same way twice. Terraform and Ansible are documentation that executes, and they make a change reviewable before it reaches production.

03What does a project look like when you start it?

Understand the data and who consumes it, get a rough version running end to end, then harden: idempotent runs, sensible retries, monitoring and a rollback path. Accuracy work comes after the pipeline is trustworthy.

04What are you learning right now?

Deeper AWS service coverage and advanced pipeline automation — mostly by building things that break in a lab rather than reading about them.
